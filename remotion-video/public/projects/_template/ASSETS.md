## Folder rules

- `fonts/`: WOFF2/WOFF font files and their license files.
- `audio/`: voice (`vo-`), music (`music-`), and effects (`sfx-`).
- `logos/`: approved brand marks and variants only.
- `images/`: photos, illustrations, screenshots, and still graphics.
- `video/`: source footage, b-roll, and generated video clips.
