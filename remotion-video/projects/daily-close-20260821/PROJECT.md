# Project brief: daily-close-20260821

## Objective

Animate the supplied daily market-close dashboard as a silent vertical clip for
Jianying/CapCut. Preserve all supplied values and wording.

## Audience and platform

- Audience: Chinese financial-market viewers
- Platform: vertical short video
- Language: Simplified Chinese
- Duration: 6 seconds
- Aspect ratio: 9:16

## Confirmed animation sequence

1. Start from black and establish the HUD framework quickly.
2. Fade in the four index cards in reading order with a subtle upward move.
3. Roll each index value rapidly to its final value; reveal its percentage.
4. Draw each intraday line from left to right.
5. Roll in the full-market turnover value `1.89 万亿元`.
6. Light up the Shanghai, Shenzhen, and Beijing components sequentially.
7. Hold the completed frame for approximately one second.

No music, voiceover, or sound effects.

## P2-P6 continuation

- P2: breadth counts, 46.67% gauge and center-out dot matrix, then limit-up/down cards.
- P3: today/yesterday turnover, contraction callout, market breakdown, then seven-period bars and line.
- P4: gainers/decliners stagger top-to-bottom, trend drawings, then heatmap illumination.
- P5: index cards and lines, turnover/breadth, focus cards, then stock rows and sparklines.
- P6: three conclusion cards, highlighted themes, then three observation rows and a one-second hold.
- Use restrained ease-out motion, low-bounce springs, rolling core numbers, and a cyan horizontal scan-line transition.
