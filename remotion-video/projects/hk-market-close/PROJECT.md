# Project brief: hk-market-close

## Objective

Turn verified Hong Kong and global market-close data into a polished, silent,
vertical Remotion video. This first deliverable is a single-screen animated
prototype for the global-markets opener.

## Audience and platform

- Audience: Chinese-speaking Hong Kong market viewers
- Platform: local vertical-video delivery
- Language: Simplified Chinese with concise English labels
- Target duration: 8 seconds for the prototype
- Required aspect ratios: 9:16 at 1080x1920

## Creative direction

- Tone: premium, precise, restrained financial HUD
- Visual references: the four user-supplied Hong Kong market boards dated
  2025-05-23; use their layout, world-map motif, cyan framing, and night skyline
  as references only
- Pacing: map establishes first, title resolves, cards stagger in, numbers roll,
  sparklines draw, then hold the completed frame
- Must include: 2026-08-28 trade date, per-market close values, directionally
  correct red/green treatment, source note, deterministic frame animation
- Must avoid: invented values, wall-clock animation, decorative motion that
  obscures data, and using a flattened reference board as the final composition

## Brand exceptions

- The supplied reference convention is used consistently: green for gains,
  red for losses, and grey for unchanged values.
- The supplied world-map/Hong Kong skyline artwork may be used as a background
  asset for this prototype.
- No logo is required.

## Deliverables

- Composition: `HKMarketCloseGlobalPrototype`
- Export profile: `vertical`
- Version: `v001`
- Audio/captions: none
